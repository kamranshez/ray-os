## GSC Position 8–20 Hack

The "hack" that's worked best for me is super unsexy but stupid effective:

1. Go into Google Search Console, filter for queries where you rank between positions 8–20 with a ton of impressions.
2. Update just those pages — add a new section that answers the "People Also Ask" questions, tweak headers to match search intent, and build 2–3 internal links pointing to it from your stronger pages.

That tiny lift can push you into the top 3, and that's where 80% of the clicks actually happen.

It beats publishing 20 new blog posts that no one ever reads.

— u/Mavericmarketer4
