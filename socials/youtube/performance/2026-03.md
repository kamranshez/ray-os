## Part 2: Revenue Audit — Stripe + PostHog Attribution

**Date pulled:** 2026-04-10
**Sources (triangulated):**
1. **Stripe charges** (succeeded, `LINK.COM* AGENTICCODIN` descriptor) via live API — bucketed into 3-day windows post-upload
2. **PostHog pageviews** (Agentic Coding School project, filter `utm_source=youtube` and `utm_campaign=<videoId>`) — measures clicks from each video's link to the masterclass site
3. **PostHog tagged purchases** (`purchase_complete` events with `utm_campaign` preserved through checkout) — lower-bound direct attribution
**Scope:** Mar 1 to Apr 3

### Methodology and its limits

Ray's masterclass description links use `?utm_source=youtube&utm_campaign=<videoId>`, so every click *should* be attributable. In practice only ~8% of March revenue (~$5,119 of $63,538 net) survives the Stripe checkout round-trip with `utm_campaign` intact. The rest is "dark" attribution: the checkout redirect loses the utm param, the buyer re-enters the site from `checkout.stripe.com`, and PostHog's `$initial_utm_campaign` on the person record ends up null.

Stripe metadata is **empty on every charge** (`metadata: {}`) so UTM data can't be recovered server-side from Stripe either.

What this means: **the 3-day Stripe window is an upper-bound attribution** (captures every charge that happened in the window, including baseline drip). **Tagged purchases are a lower-bound attribution** (only the ~8% that preserved the utm). The truth is between them. **PostHog pageviews (unique devices from each video's link)** is the most reliable signal — it measures the pitch's ability to drive clicks, independent of checkout attribution decay.

**Recommended fix:** pass `utm_campaign` explicitly into Stripe checkout session metadata on creation, then read it back from the webhook. That would raise attribution coverage from ~8% to ~80%+.

## Part 4: Hypotheses to Test

### Core Tensions from March

**1. Views are up, revenue/view is down.** Mar avg views 32.8K vs Feb 21.5K (+52%). Mar avg revenue/video $6,780 vs Feb $6,075 (+12%). The audience is growing faster than the pitch is converting. The BIRTHDAY / LASTCALL coupon is the most likely cause.

**2. The Auto Dream miss is the biggest single lesson of the month.** 97K views / $6K revenue is the worst view-to-revenue ratio of any video this year. A strong pitch with urgency on that video could plausibly have done $20K+. The opportunity cost is larger than the win on Kills OpenClaw.

**3. March 19–21 stacking failed.** Three uploads in three days overlapped their 3-day windows, doubled the content cost, and produced less combined revenue than a single well-pitched upload (Mar 11 /btw).

### H1 (carried from Feb): Masterclass-first opening with urgency
**Status:** Validated again. Mar 7 (only video with strong urgency) was the revenue winner. Mar 24 (weakest pitch on biggest audience) was the biggest miss. Continue to apply.

### H2: Retire the BIRTHDAY / LASTCALL coupon earlier
The coupon pulled AOV from ~$190 to ~$120 for most of March. Volume did not rise proportionally. March 31 AOV rebounding to $195 suggests removing the coupon (or hiding it behind a shorter deadline) would net more revenue. **Test:** pick one April video with no coupon at all, compare AOV + conversion to a matched Feb baseline.

### H3: One upload per week, not three
March's stacking experiment (Mar 19/20/21) underperformed the Feb cadence of one upload every ~5 days. **Test:** April: one upload per 5 to 7 days, measure combined reach + revenue vs any March 3-in-3-days window.

### H4: Personal discovery opening is the new working hook
"I was vibe coding and noticed..." (Mar 24) and "I woke up and checked the changelog..." (Mar 7 and 11) are the month's best hook-to-reach performers. This is Ray's version of the 3-beat outlier hook. **Rule:** every regular upload should open with a personal discovery frame, not "Anthropic just dropped X."

### H5: Always pitch with urgency on 50K+ view videos
Auto Dream proves that reach without urgency leaves money on the table. **Rule:** any video that crosses 30K views in the first 48 hours should trigger a follow-up pinned comment or closing re-edit that adds the price-escalation language.

### H6: Retire "Anthropic Just Dropped the Feature Nobody Knew They Needed"
This exact title was used twice in March (Mar 11 /btw and Mar 24 Auto Dream) and once in Feb. It still works but reuse compresses the novelty. **Test:** April, no repeat titles within 60 days.

### H7 (new, from PostHog): Fix checkout UTM persistence
Current attribution coverage is ~8%. 92% of March revenue is dark. **Engineering change:** pass `utm_campaign` into the Stripe checkout session metadata on creation, read it back on the webhook, forward it to the server-side `purchase_complete` event. Expected coverage after fix: ~80%+. **Until this is fixed, no content experiment will have clean per-video measurement.** Priority: top.

### Testing Priority (April)

1. **H7** — **Fix checkout UTM persistence.** Blocks clean measurement on every other test. Engineering, not content.
2. **H2** — Kill the BIRTHDAY / LASTCALL coupon on one April video. Measure AOV vs March avg.
3. **H4** — Apply personal-discovery opening formula to every upload.
4. **H5** — Add pinned comment with urgency to any 50K+ view April video.
5. **H3** — Limit to 5 uploads in April, not 9.
6. **H1** — Masterclass-first opening with urgency for every regular video.
7. **Double down on long-form pillar format.** Top 0.01% (41 min) had 3.16% click-through vs 0.55–1.27% for single-feature videos. Plan one more 30+ min pillar in April.
