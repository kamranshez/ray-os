## Deep Research with Exa

Demo setting up the Exa MCP server and running real business research tasks:

- Market research: "Find all SaaS companies in the accounting automation space launched in the last 12 months"
- Competitive analysis: "What are the top 5 competitors to [X] and what are their pricing models?"
- Trend spotting: "What are the emerging trends in [industry] based on recent articles?"

Show the difference between Claude Code's native web search vs Exa's specialized code/content search.

**Key demo:** Start with a blank folder, set up Exa, run 3 different research queries, and show how Claude organizes the results into structured markdown files automatically.
