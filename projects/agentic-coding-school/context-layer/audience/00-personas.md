## The 5 personas

### Persona 1 — "The Plateaued Senior" — PRIMARY TARGET
- **Who:** Staff/Senior engineer or tech lead, 5–15+ yrs, at a real software company. Real examples in the buyer base: N26 (neobank), AUTO1 (€8B used-car platform), commercetools, Trusted Shops, MCG Health, a cardiac-AI startup.
- **Current state:** Uses Claude Code/Codex *daily* (94% of audience already AI-codes; 63% daily/advanced). Competent — but plateaued. One agent, one window, retyping prompts, babysitting output.
- **Pain (their words):** "Going from prompting to automated loops" (the #1 survey challenge, 38%).
- **Wants:** To be elite / "top 0.01%." Run loops not prompts. Orchestrate agent teams. Build context layers. "Think in it."
- **Money:** High earning power, not price-sensitive. $500–600 is trivial.
- **Core objection:** "I already know Claude Code — what's left?" → Answer with the visible level above them (loops, verifiers, context engineering, orchestration).
- **This is Ray's stated ideal avatar:** already at a big company, wants to be a "superhero" with agents.
- **Copy hook:** *"You already know the tool. You're still holding it wrong."* → become an agent engineer.

### Persona 2 — "The Solo Founder / Indie Builder"
- **Who:** Founder/CTO of a small company, or solo indie. Examples: Cloud9 (13-person), Lead Odyssey (solo), ForgeFX (CTO, 20+ yrs), TakTik (AI-advisory founder), hovemunk.dk (30-yr veteran indie), DOT357 (agency owner).
- **Current state:** Ships products themselves, wears every hat.
- **Wants:** Maximum leverage. Free-text survey goals literally say *"one-man unicorn," "automate my business," "scaling via an agentic team."* Become an engineering org of one.
- **Money:** Buys **lifetime / one-time** (82% of the base does). Wants a durable asset, not a subscription.
- **Copy hook:** Agents as your team — out-ship a whole department, solo.

### Persona 3 — "The Enterprise Team" — UNTAPPED B2B LANE
- **Who:** Director/lead-led engineering-org rollout. Archetype: **MCG Health — ~60 seats**, including a **Director of Software Engineering** + multiple Staff/Senior engineers. Other multi-buyer companies: N26, commercetools, PENGINE.
- **Driver:** Raise the floor of the *whole* eng org; standardize agentic practice; team velocity. Buy is **top-down, director-approved, bulk seats.**
- **Money:** Healthcare/fintech salaries — not price-sensitive. Strongest support for premium + per-seat pricing.
- **THE GAP:** The current landing page is 100% individual. No team section, no "train your team" CTA, no seat pricing. This is real money the page doesn't speak to.
- **Copy/offer hook:** "Train your engineering team to run on agents." Seat pricing, team dashboard angle.

### Persona 4 — "The Non-Traditional Builder" — PROTECT, don't target
- **Who:** Not a career engineer. Solopreneur, career-changer, business person who wants to build real things with AI. ~12% students + a chunk of "not a traditional developer" testimonials. With beginners, ~1/3 of inbound.
- **Wants:** The "5% knowledge," but still needs an on-ramp (install, git). Values that the course "builds from the ground up."
- **Risk:** Full elite gatekeeping **alienates a cohort that converts well and leaves glowing reviews.**
- **Copy rule:** Signpost an on-ramp. "Start wherever you are, leave elite." Don't let hero copy slam the door on them. The line is **"competent → elite," not "seniors only."**

### Cross-cutting — "The Global / PPP Buyer"
- Heavily international, price-sensitive tail. PPP-discount alerts span ZA, ES, CO, TH, IN, MX, JP, KZ, BG, UA, IT, EG, NZ, HK.
- **Tension:** Premium $500–600 positioning vs auto-PPP discounting. Decide whether premium framing holds the line or keeps PPP.

### Acquisition reality (applies to all)
- **YouTube-led founder funnel.** YouTube → ~145 buyers; Google a distant second (~30). Channel @RAmjad ~39K subs. Catalog skews advanced/power-user ("Anthropic just dropped…", subagents, orchestration, "top 0.01%"). The page receives a pre-qualified, already-technical, already-curious audience.

## Positioning recommendation

**Go Direction B — but it's B-as-identity *on top of* A-as-foundation, not B instead of A.**

1. **Headline identity: "agent engineer."** Open flag (no competitor flies it), aspirational, Ray owns agentengineer.pro, and the curriculum genuinely backs it. Transformation in their words: *from prompting one agent and babysitting it → to engineering the loops, verifiers, and context layers that let agents ship reliably while you're away.*
2. **Don't gatekeep on seniority.** Keep a visible fundamentals on-ramp for Persona 4. "Competent → elite."
3. **Naming split:** lead category/SEO with **"agentic coding / agentic engineering"** (Karpathy/IBM-validated, already the brand, on-product). Use **"agent engineer" as the rallying identity word** + agentengineer.pro hero — NOT the SEO term. Reason: "agent engineer" search intent points at a *different job* (people who build agent products: LangGraph/evals).
4. **Open the team/enterprise lane** (Persona 3). It's real money the page ignores.
5. **$500–600 is supported** but sits just above the bootcamp floor ($399–499) and well below the identity-premium tier ($995–1,600). Price and identity are linked — the identity must earn the step up.
6. **"Always current" is a real moat.** Top-2 desire (keeping up) + the entire YouTube catalog is release-reaction. "The only course updated the day Anthropic ships" differentiates vs static $995 cohorts.

### The villain (candidate, validate with Ray)
*"You already know Claude Code. But you're still babysitting one agent in one window, retyping the same prompts — and you've plateaued."* Mirrors aihero's "two errors" structure but grounded in this audience's actual #1 pain.
