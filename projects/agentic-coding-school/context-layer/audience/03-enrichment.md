## Aggregate read
- ~4/9 of the individual sample are founders/C-level; ~3/9 senior/mid technical; the rest mid/junior or unidentified.
- Confidence-weighted: ~57% senior pros at real companies (high-confidence sample).
- Geographic skew: Europe-heavy (Germany ×4, Estonia, Slovenia, plus US, Nordics, Canada, Central America).
- Technical depth is high: API platforms, cloud-native SaaS, fintech infra, AI systems, regulated environments (banking, law, healthcare).
- **Notable:** at least two buyers are *already* publicly positioning around "Agentic Engineering" (Trusted Shops' Whelehan, commercetools ecosystem) — the term is landing with exactly this crowd.

## Caveats
- ~25–33% of the individual sample unidentified/low-confidence — noisier than "pure Fortune 500 architects."
- Roles partly inferred from email domain, not stated titles.
- Freemail buyers (the 70%) largely not enrichable from a first name + gmail; this analysis covers the corporate/custom-domain slice only.
