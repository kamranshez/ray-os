# Pricing

The price points, tiers, refund terms, and policies. The narrative justification lives in `positioning/brief.md`; this is the reference for the actual numbers and rules. Open price questions are tracked in `positioning/open-decisions.md`.

## The offer (DECIDED 2026-06-27)
One clean, **growing all-access pass**: buy once, get every class in the school, **including future ones**, kept current forever. No expansion/upgrade tier (it was dropped; its "updates are the upgrade" rationale died once updates became free with the class).

- **Individual: monthly / yearly / lifetime**, each granting the whole catalog, current and future, kept current forever. Lifetime is the anchor and the hero.
- **Team: 5+ seats**, volume tiers, invoice/PO billing. A Team Stripe product already exists. This is the B2B lane (sold as a "talk to us" band, not self-serve).

## Price level & the launch ladder (DECIDED 2026-06-27)
- Current lifetime anchor: **~$397**.
- **The price ratchets up one step per brand-new-class launch.** Each launch is a higher rung. Earlier buyers keep the price they locked; the raise hits new buyers from the sale's end. The old "~$397 to ~$500 once Loopy AI ships" is now just the first rungs of this ladder, not a one-off milestone.
- **Per-launch sale:** when a brand-new class goes live, run a **~2-week sale (~30% off / the old anchor held)**, framed as a price step-up: "the new class is in, the price goes up when this sale ends, buy now to lock today's price forever." Real dated raise, not a standing discount. Targets new buyers (existing holders already get the new class free).
- Step size per rung is set per launch (round-number targets), not fixed.

## Refund terms
- **Monthly: no refund.**
- **Yearly / lifetime: 14-day money-back guarantee.**

## Policies
- **Future classes are INCLUDED in all-access** (decided 2026-06-27, reversing the 2026-05-08 "separate products" policy). Buy once, get every future class too. Landing copy SHOULD say "buy once, get everything, including what's coming." The price ladder (above) is how new classes are monetized: they raise the anchor for future buyers, not re-sold to the existing base. See `decisions/2026-06-27-monetization-and-launch-cadence.md`.
- **PPP (purchasing-power parity):** an auto-discount currently runs for eligible international visitors. Whether to hold the line at full price (premium feel) or keep the discount is OPEN. It's a runtime banner, so it doesn't block copy.

## Non-goals
- Do NOT clone IndyDevDan's two-product split (cheap "principled" + expensive "tactical"). Ray's funnel is smaller and $397 already sits near Dan's midpoint; a cheap tier would cannibalize. Sequence a separate on-ramp product later if ever.
