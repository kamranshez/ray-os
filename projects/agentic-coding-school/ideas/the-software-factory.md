## Retroactive

Runs on a schedule.

- Requires event tracking or screen recordings from stuff like PostHog or Microsoft Clarity to come up with informed decisions.
  - You want it to give you different proposals. Some normal. Some extreme.
  - It may do simple fixes or move things around to make it less confusing and remove rage clicks.
- Sentry would be for fixing simple bugs.

## Proactive

Note that more features isn't always better.

### Data sources — deciding what to build

- Monitoring competitors.
- Market trends. YouTube videos. Industry podcasts. Etc.
- User feedback.

### Execution

- It either makes a proposal that you approve or just makes a prototype. Depending on your token spend.
- Auto specs out the feature. Deploys the environment for you to test if you want to.
- Deploys the changes.
