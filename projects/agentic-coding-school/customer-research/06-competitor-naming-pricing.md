# 06 — Competitor Positioning, Term Maturity & Pricing

**Source:** Exa web research. Pulled 2026-06-26.

## Term maturity
- **"AI engineer" — fully mature, but owned & generic.** Coined by swyx (Latent Space, Jun 2023); anchors the AI Engineer conference ecosystem (6,000+ at SF World's Fair, AIE Europe, 400k+ subscribers), "one of the fastest-growing job titles." Max search volume + legitimacy, but broad and the flag is effectively owned. Leading here = crowded, generic lane vs an incumbent movement.
- **"Agentic coding / agentic engineering" — emerging but fast-rising, and the precise label for this product.** Karpathy publicly named "agentic engineering" as successor to "vibe coding" (~Feb–Apr 2026, Sequoia AI Ascent). IBM + a stream of vendors (Leanware, Glide, Doshby, GAP, sdd.sh) formalizing it within weeks. ~6 months old — textbook "still shapeable" term. **Already Ray's brand** ("Agentic Coding School") and on-product.
- **"Agent engineer" — a real, rising job title, BUT mostly denotes a DIFFERENT job.** KEY FINDING. Job postings, salary bands ($185K–$320K), 340% YoY posting-growth claims, self-titled LinkedIn profiles. **However** in the job market it means *someone who builds autonomous agent products* (LangGraph, multi-agent orchestration, RAG, evals) — NOT someone who uses coding agents to ship everyday software. Search intent points at the agent-builder career. There's also a softer identity usage (Doshby: "developers half-jokingly started rebranding themselves 'agent engineers'" after Karpathy). Two meanings; the higher-volume one is a partial audience mismatch.

**Pattern:** premium players all flag an *engineer identity* — "Real Engineers," "AI-Native Builder," "agentic engineer." **Nobody leads with "agent engineer" as their banner.** The winning educator word is "engineer / agentic engineering," and "agent engineer" is an **open flag.**

## Price anchors
- **Commodity / live-bootcamp:** $399–499 (Claude Camp, Luca Berton).
- **Mid:** ~$500–600 — currently a relatively **empty band.**
- **Premium cohort:** $995 (Pocock), €1,490 (~$1,600), $1,499 (Avthar).

**$500–600 sits low-mid** — above the bootcamp floor, below every identity-flagged premium cohort. Defensible **only if positioning earns the step up from $499.** Price and identity flag are linked (the "Real Engineers" identity is what lets Pocock charge $995). At $500–600 with a credible identity flag, you're *underpricing* the premium tier — a reasonable, not aggressive, bet.

## The open lane (recommendation)
**Lead category/SEO with "agentic coding / agentic engineering"; use "agent engineer" as the aspirational identity/outcome flag — not the primary search term.**
1. "AI engineer" — taken, generic, hard to rank.
2. "Agent engineer" as a *primary search anchor* is risky — dominant intent = the agent-builder career (mismatch), still emerging (you'd be creating the meaning). Don't bet SEO on it.
3. "Agentic coding/engineering" — established-enough to search, Karpathy/IBM-validated, still young to co-own, exactly the product, already the brand. Low-risk SEO anchor.
4. "Agent engineer" = best *identity flag* (the "Real Engineers" equivalent). "Become an agent engineer" / "the agent engineer's path." You own agentengineer.pro; no competitor flies it; justifies the premium. The rallying word on the page, not the URL you rank for.

**In short: don't bet the SEO on "agent engineer"; bet the *identity* on it.**

## Caveats
- Strong recency (most sources Feb–Jun 2026).
- aihero $995 = list (templated price placeholders on official page; confirmed via two listing mirrors); launch-week sale lower.
- Salary/posting-growth figures from career-marketing blogs — directional, not audited. Qualitative signal (agent engineer = fast-emerging real title) well-corroborated; specific numbers softer.
- The two-meanings split for "agent engineer" is inference but well-supported — worth validating with real keyword/SERP data before committing the domain to it as an SEO term.
