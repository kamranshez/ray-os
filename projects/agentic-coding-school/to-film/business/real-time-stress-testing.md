## Real-Time Stress Testing Your Work

Use a second Claude session as a live adversarial reviewer while you're actively drafting — catching contradictions, over-promises, and blind spots before they leave your desk.

> "I ran each draft paragraph through Claude to pressure-test whether any of my arguments had unintended implications for other provisions in the agreement. It caught one: a defense I was planning to raise on the service-level metrics could have been read to concede a point on the payment dispute in Section 7. I rewrote the response."
> — [Zack Shapiro (@zackbshapiro)](https://x.com/zackbshapiro/status/2027389987444957625)

### What to Cover

1. **The pattern** — Two sessions running side by side. One creates, the other critiques. You decide what to keep.

   > "That kind of real-time, provision-by-provision stress-testing while actively drafting is something that used to require a second lawyer reviewing your work. Now it happens in the same conversation where the work gets done."
   > — [Zack Shapiro (@zackbshapiro)](https://x.com/zackbshapiro/status/2027389987444957625)

2. **Demo: drafting a client proposal** — Main session drafts sections of a proposal. After each section, feed it to a review session that checks for:
   - Internal contradictions between sections
   - Scope creep (promising things not in the pricing)
   - Pricing conflicts with deliverables
   - Ambiguous language that could be read against you
   - Missing caveats or assumptions

3. **The cross-reference catch** — The most powerful thing about this pattern is catching where one part of your document undermines another. The lawyer's example: his defense on one provision would have accidentally conceded a point on a completely different section. A human reviewer might catch this. A subagent reviewing the full document *always* catches it.

4. **Applicable everywhere** — Contracts, proposals, marketing copy, business plans, investor decks, policy documents — anything where internal consistency matters and the stakes are high enough to warrant a second pair of eyes.

### Key Demo

Draft a 3-section proposal live. Show the review session catching a real contradiction between the scope section and the pricing section that you "accidentally" introduced. Fix it in real time. Show how the review loop takes seconds, not the hours it would take to get a colleague's feedback.
