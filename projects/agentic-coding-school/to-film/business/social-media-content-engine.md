## Social Media Content Engine

Build a content production system using Claude Code:

1. **Brand voice skill** — Create a skill file that captures your tone, style, vocabulary, and content pillars
2. **Content calendar** — Generate a month of content ideas based on your pillars + current trends (via Exa)
3. **Batch generation** — Use headless mode to generate all posts at once, formatted per platform (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram captions)
4. **Review workflow** — Output drafts to a folder, review in any text editor, approve or request revisions

**Key demo:** Go from zero to 30 days of social content in under 10 minutes.
