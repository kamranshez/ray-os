# Self-Annealing Workflows

Progressively improving AI workflows over time. Inspired by metallurgical annealing (heating metal → lowest-energy crystal state). Start rough → run → measure → adjust → repeat. Over iterations the workflow becomes highly optimized.

Applied to business processes (cold email, proposals, lead scoring) not just code metrics. Difference from auto research. Safety guardrails as workflows become more autonomous.
