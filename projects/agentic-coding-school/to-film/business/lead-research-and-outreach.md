## Lead Research & Outreach

Use Claude Code + Exa to find and research potential clients:

1. **Find prospects** — Search for companies/people matching your ideal customer profile
2. **Research each one** — Pull their website, recent content, company info
3. **Personalized outreach** — Generate custom email drafts based on research
4. **Output** — Structured folder with one file per prospect (research + draft email)

**Key demo:** "Find 10 e-commerce companies in Australia with 10-50 employees that don't have an active blog" → research each → draft personalized cold emails.

Relevant to John Kim's marketing business.
