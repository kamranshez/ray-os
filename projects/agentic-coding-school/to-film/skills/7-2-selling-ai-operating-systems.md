## Selling AI Operating Systems

The system you just built is what businesses pay $15K+ for. How to productize and sell it.

### The Pitch

"I'll build an AI operating system that replaces 3-4 employees for your business for a one-time fee."

From solopreneur video: "I'm working with three clients right now to replace their marketing teams with my exact AI employees. And the average deal size is $15,000. These are all US clients."

"When clients are willing to pay tens of thousands of dollars for this type of AI operating system, I call this smart." (Solopreneur video)

### The Market

"Look at all the companies that are eight figures, nine figures. If you're able to reduce their expenses by even 70%, you're able to profit multiple six to seven figures." (Solopreneur video)

From 1% framework: "If you're building an agency, skills become totally productizable. You can build a skill once, deploy it for multiple clients, customize it slightly, and probably charge much higher rates."

### The Delivery

1. **Skill plugin** — packaged set of skills for the client's business
2. **Onboarding session** — install, configure connectors, test each skill
3. **Customization** — use the customizer pattern (Ch 5.5) to tailor to their brand/voice
4. **Workflow visualizer** — show the client their complete system (Ch 6.3)

### The Recurring Angle

Monthly retainer for:
- Skill maintenance and updates
- New skills as business evolves
- Learnings file curation
- Auto-improvement suggestions (Ch 5.4) → new skills each month

### The Closer

"Anyone can go and install 280,000 generic skills. But building one that's tailored to a specific business with years of knowledge and context — that's where the real value is." (1% framework)

"This is the biggest opportunity of 2026. I'm literally working with 190+ entrepreneurs one-on-one helping them build their own AI operating systems and they're bringing in deals in the five-figure range." (Solopreneur video)

The class started with "build skills for yourself." It ends with "sell skills to others."
