## The Morning Briefing Employee

The skill that demonstrates the "AI employee" concept most viscerally. Plus the update skill as its bookend.

### The Morning Briefing

Build a skill that runs every morning with everything you need to start your day.

From Brock's video: "Every single morning at 7 or 8am, it sends me a morning briefing with everything on my calendar, specific industry news, and even pulls through my email inbox to find emails I need to reply to that are urgent."

### What to Show

1. Build the morning briefing skill with Skill Creator
2. Connect to Gmail, Google Calendar via connectors
3. Show the HTML dashboard output — calendar, emails to respond to, suggested replies
4. "This is basically a dashboard with all the stuff on my plate for today. Shows my schedule, specific emails I need to respond to, and even suggested replies." (Brock)
5. Turn it into a scheduled task (daily at 7am)

### The Bookend — The Update Skill

The morning briefing starts your day. The update skill ends it.

From Lenny's video: The "update skill" that syncs everything after real-world work. "Once I get through that first milestone and I'm in the chat telling it what I've done, updating it on the trials and tribulations — I run this update skill so it'll update everything so we can stay in sync."

Build both in this video — natural complements. Morning = what's ahead. Evening = what happened.

### Connectors Intro

"Co-work can do some amazing things, but if it doesn't have access to the apps you use every day — email, Google Calendar, Slack — it just makes it that much better." (Brock)

Show the connectors panel: Gmail, Calendar, Notion, Slack, Canva, HubSpot, etc.

### Zapier MCP Hack

For apps not in the native list: "I use Zapier MCP because they have 8,000+ apps preconfigured. Click new MCP server, select Claude Co-work, add tools — now Claude can perform tasks across Stripe, create payment links, pull payment details, all that stuff." (Brock)
