## Sharing Skills with Your Team

Package skills for distribution. Turn years of expertise into files anyone can use from day one.

### The Mentorship Bottleneck

"Senior expertise normally transfers through years of osmosis: shadowing, feedback loops, institutional memory. Skills compress that into a file." (skills-as-team-knowledge brief)

### Distribution Methods

**For technical teams**: Commit skills to a shared repo — anyone who clones gets the skills. Project-scope skills travel with the codebase.

**For non-technical teams**: Plugin bundles. "You can combine a bunch of different skills and share them with your team or even plug them into your own Claude experience." (Brock)

Brock's example: zip file of 15 skills → download → Customize → Plugins → Add plugin → upload zip → all 15 skills installed.

**For enterprises**: managed-settings.json — deploy skills across an organization account.

### Consistent Output Demo

Show: two Claude instances, same skill, same input → consistent output. vs blank Claude → wildly different output.

"The plugin is transferable. If I had 50 associates, I could install it on every machine. Every associate would immediately produce contract reviews using my analytical framework, draft communications in my voice, and apply tracked changes in my preferred format." — Zack Shapiro

### The Quality Floor

"The output still requires human review, but the review starts from a much higher baseline." — Zack Shapiro

Skills don't replace oversight. They raise the starting point.
