## Scheduling Skills as Autonomous Agents

Any skill can become a scheduled task. The skill runs without you.

### Core Concept

From Brock: "Any skill we create, we can turn into a recurring task. Every hour, every 3 hours, once a day, weekly, end of the month."

From solopreneur video: "I use a schedule ability in Claude which will run my employees on a set day at a set time. Every Sunday when I open my laptop I have all the week's to-dos, videos, finances there."

### What to Show

1. Take the morning briefing → schedule daily at 7am
2. Take the receipt scanner → schedule weekly (Friday 5pm)
3. Show the scheduled tasks dashboard — all "employees" and when they run

**Setting it up** (from Brock):
- Either say "Turn this into a scheduled task" in chat
- Or click schedule button → "I want this to run every Friday at 5pm"
- It asks for confirmation → click schedule
- "Now you can see every single day at 5pm only on Fridays this will run." (Brock)

**Delivery**: Show output arriving via Slack or Telegram. "It will send me a message in Slack with an HTML dashboard." (Brock)

### The Sunday CEO Pattern

"I wake up. I open Claude Co-work. I check what needs to happen this week. I tell my content director to plan the next batch of videos. I tell my LinkedIn director to draft this week's posts. I review the financial summary from last month. I have all these employees working on a Sunday because I use schedule." (Solopreneur video)

"So now I can just focus on the strategy, the high-level decisions and the relationships. This AI operating system is what businesses and enterprises are spending tens of thousands of dollars for." (Solopreneur video)
