## The Operations Manager

Build operational skills for the boring stuff that eats your time.

### Receipt Scanner

From Brock's video: "I have this folder on my desktop with a bunch of random receipts. Claude pulled and read those receipts, added it to this interface, breaks down grand total. First output: a spreadsheet generated automatically. Second: an expense report dashboard — total spend, average price per receipt, total tax paid."

Then turn it into a scheduled task: "I want this to run every single Friday at 5pm at the end of the week." (Brock)

### Invoice Generator

"I used to use an invoice generating software and it was a pain. Now I have a skill that knows exactly how I want my invoices. I just say 'generate an invoice for Bob, $75K AI contracting, come up with the rest.' It generates a PDF with all my information, invoice number, date, due date, payment terms." (Brock)

Elliot's version: "I hate writing and creating invoices. I don't even have to log into my tax software anymore. Just tell Claude to do it — got my logo, colors, literally in my font, billing address, everything ready to go."

### What to Show

1. **Receipt scanner**: Drop receipts into a folder → skill categorizes, generates spreadsheet + HTML dashboard
2. **Invoice generator**: One sentence → formatted PDF with all business details pre-filled from brand context
3. **Schedule the receipt scanner**: Every Friday at 5pm

### The Financial Reporting Angle

"I'd rather build five apps than spend 30 minutes looking at a spreadsheet. So I built a financial reporting skill that handles all the finances for my company. It's like my own personal income tax adviser." (Solopreneur video)

"Is this an obvious replacement for a CFO or accountant? No. But it helps keep things financially organized better than if you had no one doing it or better than paying someone $65K a year." (Solopreneur video)

### Budget Planner Bonus

From Brock: An interactive financial dashboard where you can change income and it adjusts savings projections, percentage saved, rent proportion, month-by-month savings forecast.
