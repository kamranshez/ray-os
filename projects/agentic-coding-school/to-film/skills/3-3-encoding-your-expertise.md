## Encoding Your Expertise

"Templates are commodities, judgment isn't." Your years of expertise is exactly the asset AI makes more valuable.

### The Problem

"Most lawyers who try AI write something like 'review this contract' and get back something mediocre. Then they decide AI isn't useful for legal work. The problem is not the AI. The problem is the input." — Zack Shapiro

This applies to every profession. Generic prompt = generic output.

### The Before/After

- **Bad**: "review this contract"
- **Good**: "Review this services agreement from the vendor's perspective. Flag provisions where the customer shifted risk beyond market norms. Check for missing provisions including limitation of liability, IP ownership, data handling, and termination for convenience. Produce a severity-rated summary with specific counter-language for each high-severity issue."

"The entire gap between 'AI is a toy' and 'AI changed my practice' lives in the quality of your instructions." — Zack Shapiro

### What to Show — Live Build

Take a professional workflow the viewer does weekly and build a skill that encodes their analytical framework:

**Example: Contract Reviewer Skill**
- Red flags (clauses that could hurt you)
- Yellow flags (worth reviewing)
- Missing terms
- Bottom-line verdict (sign / negotiate / walk)
- Negotiation suggestions with specific counter-language

From Brock's video: "It generated this markdown file breaking down every single thing inside the contract — short version, key terms, red flags, yellow flags, what's missing, bottom line whether I should sign it, and things I should negotiate."

### The Key Insight

"Experienced lawyers have an enormous advantage in this new world, and most of them don't realize it. If you've spent 10 or 20 years developing judgment in your practice area, you are sitting on exactly the asset that AI makes more valuable, not less." — Zack Shapiro

This applies to any domain expert — consultants, marketers, accountants, project managers.
