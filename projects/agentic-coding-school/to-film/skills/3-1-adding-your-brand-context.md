## Adding Your Brand Context

The personalization video. Takes output from "sounds like AI" to "sounds like me."

### Core Concept

A skill without your business context is a commodity. The same research skill with your brand voice, audience profile, and positioning baked in produces dramatically different — and better — output.

"An AI SEO skill that knows your brand voice, your product offering, your audience, your content pillars, your competitors, and your positioning will produce content that will both rank and bring you traffic for relevant queries." (7 Levels)

### What to Show

**Create three reference files** in a shared `brand-context/` folder:

1. **voice-tone.md** — How you communicate. "For me it's conversational, helpful, confident but not arrogant. I use real examples instead of theory." (Brand DNA video). Just 3-4 bullet points.

2. **visual-style.md** — Color palette, font choices, imagery style. Can include actual brand asset files (logos, fonts, PDFs). "My primary colors are blue and white. Clean sans-serif fonts. Modern and minimalist imagery." (Brand DNA video)

3. **messaging-pillars.md** — 3-4 core messages everything ties back to. "For me: AI makes you more productive, anyone can learn this, I show you how not just talk about it." (Brand DNA video)

**Add "context needs" section** to the research skill's skill.md pointing to these files. Show the skill loading only what it needs per step.

**Before/after comparison**: Same research prompt with and without brand context.

### The Brand Applicator Approach (Elliot's Method)

For deeper brand encoding, build a standalone "brand identity guardian" skill:
1. Point Co-work at a folder with actual brand assets (logos, fonts, brand guideline PDF)
2. Claude extracts hex codes, approved color pairings, typography, logo placement rules
3. Builds the skill automatically — way deeper than text descriptions
4. "It's got my color system extracted, approved color pairings, typography, all logo files, even output recipes for how to apply in HTML, React dashboards, PDF reports." (Elliot)

Result: "Any document, LinkedIn post, PDF — just tell it to apply your brand and it gets your colors, fonts, everything." (Elliot)

### The Skill = Craft Applied to Knowledge

"A copywriter knows how to write a great sales email. That is the skill. You can apply that across whole sets of knowledge. You teach the skill once and then you can apply it to any set of knowledge." (Elliot)
