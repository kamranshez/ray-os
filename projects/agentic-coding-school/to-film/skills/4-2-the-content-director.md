## The Content Director

Chain multiple skills into one workflow. The "content director" pattern.

### Core Concept

From solopreneur video: "When I tell Claude 'plan and script my next batch of videos,' here's what actually happens. First the ideation skill kicks in — researches competitor channels, finds what's trending, calculates view multipliers, generates nine video ideas. Then the script writing skill takes over — writes full scripts in my exact voice, sentence structure, expressions, pacing. Then it organizes everything into a content calendar with upload dates."

"All that is done with just one command. That's not a tool. That's a content director."

### What to Build

1. **Ideation skill** — research trending topics, competitor analysis, view multiplier calculation
2. **Scriptwriting skill** — your voice, format, pacing (pulls from brand-context/)
3. **Calendar skill** — organizes into publishing calendar with dates and order

### Auto-Routing

Key point: you don't say "use skill A then B then C."

"Claude Co-work is smart enough to automatically figure out which skills to use based on what you're asking. You just describe the task and co-work looks at all your active skills and decides which ones apply." (Solopreneur video)

### Visual Output Demo

Don't just show text. Include a visual skill in the chain — slide deck builder or branded proposal.

"This entire slideshow you are seeing in this video was generated with this specific skill. And I'm going to show you how easy it is." (Brock)

Show the content director producing a script AND a presentation deck for the same video, using brand context from Ch 3.
