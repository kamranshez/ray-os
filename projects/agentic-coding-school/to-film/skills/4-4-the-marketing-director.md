## The Marketing Director

LinkedIn posts, email drafts, SEO content — all with your positioning and ICP baked in.

### LinkedIn Skill

From solopreneur video: "I built a LinkedIn director that runs my entire LinkedIn strategy. It generates 10 to 12 post ideas based on what's trending, what drives leads to my agency, and what positions me as authority in my niche. Then it writes two variations of every single post in my exact voice, and organizes them into a posting calendar — all from one command."

### Email Drafter

"Whenever I have cold outreach, follow-ups, or specific negotiations I need help with, it automatically creates a specific email tailored to any conversation. The best part: you can connect it to Gmail so it pulls all your emails, summarizes them, and drafts responses." (Brock)

### All Pulling from Brand Context

Every marketing skill uses the shared `brand-context/` folder from Ch 3:
- voice-tone.md → posts sound like you
- messaging-pillars.md → content ties back to your core messages
- icp.md → targeted to your actual audience

### The Numbers

"Before Claude, I was spending roughly $4,500 a month on help — part-time copywriter $2,500, creative director $1,000, freelancers $1,000. That's $54,000 a year gone. Now my entire operation runs on Claude Max, $100 a month. That's a 97.8% reduction in operational cost." (Solopreneur video)

### Research Skill with Scoring

From Brand DNA video: Research skill that scores topics 1-10, only searches last 30 days, cross-references with content pillars. "Find me five trending topics about AI automation — five topics back with scores and angles. Topic three scored a nine."
