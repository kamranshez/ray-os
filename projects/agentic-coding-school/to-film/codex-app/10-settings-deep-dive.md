## Settings Deep Dive

This video is about turning Codex from a cool demo into a daily tool.

Settings are not flashy, but they decide whether Codex feels smooth or annoying: sleep behavior, notifications, approvals, sandboxing, custom instructions, memory, dictation, editor defaults, appearance, and personality.

## Big Picture

The settings page is the control panel for friction.

Students should leave understanding which settings affect safety, which affect interruptions, and which affect personal taste.

## Teaching Line

> Settings are not flashy, but they decide whether Codex feels annoying or useful as a daily tool.

## Visual Variations

### Variation 1: Control Panel For Friction

Big idea: show settings as knobs that tune daily Codex behavior.

Core labels: sleep, notifications, approvals, editor, appearance.

### Variation 2: Safety vs Convenience

Big idea: show approvals and sandboxing as a balance between speed and control.

Core labels: ask first, auto-run, sandbox, trust level.

### Variation 3: Daily Driver Setup

Big idea: show a clean Codex setup with the right defaults for long-running work.

Core labels: prevent sleep, notify me, custom instructions, memory.
