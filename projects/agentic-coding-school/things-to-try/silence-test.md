**The silence test.** Before ending a session, ask: *"is there anything I haven't asked that I should have?"*

Models are trained to answer the question you asked, not the question you should have asked. They sit on context they noticed but didn't volunteer because nothing prompted them to. A missing edge case, a config that looks suspicious, a test that probably won't pass on CI. They saw it. You didn't ask. So it stays silent.

The silence test breaks that. It explicitly invites the model to surface what it's been holding back. The output is often the most valuable thing in the whole session.

Pair it with: *"what would a senior engineer reviewing this notice that I missed?"* Same mechanism, slightly different framing, sometimes pulls different things out.
