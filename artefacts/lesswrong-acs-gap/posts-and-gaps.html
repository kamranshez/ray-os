Findings that drove the design

  • The 2,000-character cap was the entire recall bottleneck. plaintextDescription and plaintextMainText are both hard-capped; contents { markdown } is not. The best post found was unreachable by any title-and-intro filter by construction — its title says "empirical research", its intro is disclaimers, and every technique sits past the cap.
  • search_videos has silent recall failure, proven three times. It was fed a video's own agentContext and did not return that video. No pagination: exactly 10 results, always, out of 362. So absence from the top 10 is not evidence of absence, and no "net-new" verdict can rest on search alone.
  • Much of the 362-video catalog is plan, not filmed library. One query collapsed from 10 hits to 1 under completedOnly. "Already covered" therefore often means "already on the plan", which is completely different advice and needs its own verdict tag.
  • Rank by density, not counts. Raw counts rank by length. The shipped gate (density ≥ 3 / ≥ 6 / karma ≥ 10) was chosen by measuring recall against 10 known-good posts — 10/10, versus 7/10 for the tightest gate tried.
  • Alignment Forum needs no separate fetch. All 49 AF posts in the window are already in the LW corpus. Zero AF-only. Saves a whole track.
