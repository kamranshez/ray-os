What the shape says judgment

Referral is the crown jewel — the only holding that is both high-volume and high-depth (3,649 visitors at 4.4 views each, 16,066 pageviews, more than any other channel). Whoever is linking you is sending people who stay.

Organic Video is a leaky bucket. 1,975 visitors at 1.0 views each — they land and leave. It is your loudest channel by reach and your shallowest by engagement.

Direct is big but passive. 5,400 visitors is the largest holding, but it is a lagging index of everything else (brand, returns, bookmarks) — hard to move by pushing on it directly.

Email is small but dense. 912 visitors, but they are your owned, highest-intent audience — the question is whether it deserves far more than its 6.6% share.
