Reading the shape honestly

Why the money math stops where it does.

The funnel is non-monotonic. /auth/callback (500) and /member (1,117) both exceed /auth/sign-up (475). A true new-user funnel can only shrink. The bulge is the fingerprint of 2,631 returning members signing back in through /auth/sign-in and merging into the same pages — so only landing → sign-up can be trusted as a real acquisition rate.
What that means for decisions. The defensible money path is / → /auth/sign-up → purchase. Everything past sign-up is contaminated and can't be scored for new revenue. You can switch the model to the literal 4-stage page funnel below, but its downstream rates exceed 100% and its revenue figure is not trustworthy — it's there to show you why.
