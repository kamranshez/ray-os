## Primary: Ray's Own Newsletters

**These are the main calibration source. Read them first.** They are Ray's real sends, in his actual voice and length: short, declarative, opinion-led, often pure-value with no pitch. When you draft, match these for length and register, not the longer Ali examples. Full text and assets are stored in `examples/` subdirectories by date, each with a metadata header and KEY PATTERNS notes for performance tracking.
