## Face Reference Photos

Add 3-5 high-quality photos of your face here for consistent thumbnail generation.

### Best reference photos include:
- **Front-facing** — straight-on, good lighting
- **3/4 profile** — slightly turned, shows face shape
- **Surprised/excited** — exaggerated expression (mouth open, wide eyes)
- **Neutral/serious** — calm, professional look
- **Smiling** — genuine, warm smile

### Requirements:
- Clear, well-lit photos
- Head-and-shoulders framing (not full body)
- No sunglasses or heavy filters
- Variety of expressions helps the model understand your face from multiple angles
- JPG or PNG format

### File naming:
Use descriptive names like:
- `front-neutral.jpg`
- `three-quarter-smile.jpg`
- `front-surprised.jpg`
