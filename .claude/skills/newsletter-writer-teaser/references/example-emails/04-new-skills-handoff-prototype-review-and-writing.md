Hey [name],
Two new skills just landed in my repo, and they’re both solving
real problems I was running into.

The first is /handoff. It solves this awkward problem I kept
running into: you’re deep in a grilling session, your context
window is filling up, and suddenly you need to pivot and
prototype something or fix a bug. You could squeeze that work
into your remaining tokens, but it feels cramped and wrong.

With /handoff, you can just hand off the entire conversation to a
fresh agent - context, vibe, and all - and spin up a completely
separate workspace. Fix your thing. Then hand back with what you
learned. It’s like having a genuinely autonomous sub-agent,
except you stay in control the whole time.

The second is /prototype. It’s for those moments when you have an
unknown unknown - something you can only figure out by building
it. For UI work, it generates radically different variations and
lets you mix and match the best bits. For state machines and
business logic? Same idea, but it builds an interactive terminal
app instead. The trick is getting a human in the loop to apply
taste, because AI often can’t see what it’s building.

I’ve also fixed some bugs and I’m sneaking a preview of some
in-progress stuff: a three-part writing skill (fragments -> beats
-> shape) that treats prototyping as a writing technique, and a
/review skill that checks code against both your repo’s standards
and the original spec.
