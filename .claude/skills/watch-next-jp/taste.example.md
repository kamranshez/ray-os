# taste.md — the legible taste digest (optional)

A short, hand-editable prose profile the /recommend skill reads alongside
`taste.json`. `taste.json` is the structured signal; this file is the plain-
English summary you can correct in one place. Keep it honest to the data —
don't invent preferences your ratings don't show. Delete this template's
guidance and write your own.

## You reliably rate high
- Calm, curious, learnable content — walking vlogs, explainers, quiet
  documentary-ish footage with a clear narrator.
- (example) 散歩 / 秘境 / 廃墟 / 限界ニュータウン veins.
- Solo presenters, mid-register, unhurried delivery, dry humor.

## You bail on
- Loud engagement-bait, drama, rage-clickbait.
- (example) overlong 総集編 compilations unless the presenter carries them.

## Ambiguous / revisit-when-stronger
- Things you enjoyed but couldn't fully follow — keep the topic, don't read the
  low comprehension as a taste-negative.

## Presenter taste (rolled up from channel fingerprints)
- "Calm, clear, mid-register solo explainers with dry humor" — match this
  against unknown channels to discover new ones, not just resurface known ones.
