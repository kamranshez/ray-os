**Channel-level view performance (the real story):** First 2h47m, this video ranked **9 of 10** in the channel's "Top recent videos" panel at **1.5k views** — *below* the channel's typical band (1.9k–3.7k), with the explicit YouTube flag **"Fewer subscribers are choosing to watch this video."** For comparison, same window: /dream 9.0k, Subagents 2.0 7.3k, /workflows ("Update Everyone's Obsessed") 6.6k, /goal ("Going Viral Right Now") 2.4k. **This is a CTR/impressions problem, not a watch-share problem** — the A/B watch-time spread (7pt) is healthy, but the title+thumbnail package isn't converting impressions into clicks at the channel baseline.

**Key takeaways:**

- **Concrete benefit beats discovery-curiosity on a Codex video.** Ranking is concrete-outcome ("Manages All Your Agents", 37.1%) > vague-change ("Changes How You Run Agents", 33%) > mystery ("Nobody Knew They Needed", 30%). The exact opposite of where the V14 "Nobody Knew They Needed" formula sits on Anthropic videos (46.8% all-time #2).

- **V14's "Nobody Knew They Needed" FLOORED at 30% here — important boundary on that formula.** It is the worst of the three. Two compounding causes: (a) the formula's power on this channel came bundled with the **"Anthropic Just Dropped"** authority anchor — stripped of it ("The New Codex Feature Nobody Knew They Needed"), the discovery hook loses its institutional weight; (b) **redundancy with the thumbnail** — "Nobody Knew They Needed" sells mystery while the thumbnail already announces "Codex Runs Itself", spoiling the gap. **Banked: the "Nobody Knew They Needed" formula does NOT transfer to non-Anthropic (Codex) videos — it needs the authority anchor to carry it.**

- **All three titles shared the same "The New Codex Update/Feature..." opener — low frame diversity.** Per the skill's all-similar-variants anti-pattern, this round couldn't test genuinely different frames. Ray noted he "combined the previous winners together" — the merge collapsed three distinct formulas into one generic opener, so the 7pt spread is really testing the *suffix* (benefit vs change vs mystery), not the *frame*.

- **The thumbnail is the prime suspect for the low views.** "Codex Runs Itself" + an abstract three-tile tree is harder to parse at feed size than V26's clean **"/goal"** folder (a recognizable slash command). V26 with the same Codex-folder system but a crisp `/goal` label pulled 2.4k in the same window and hit 39.1% watch-share. The director-tree diagram reads as visual noise at thumbnail scale. **Strong candidate for a thumbnail re-test, not just a title re-test.**

### Recommendation (next round)

The winning lever was **concrete benefit/outcome**, and the format-ceiling risk is real (Codex, no Anthropic anchor). R2 should (a) keep the concrete-benefit winner, (b) re-introduce the V26-proven Codex **social-proof** frame that the merge dropped, and (c) test a sharper concrete-mechanic frame. Also strongly consider swapping the thumbnail.

1. **The New Codex Update That Manages All Your Agents** — R1 leader, control (~37%)
2. **The Codex Feature Everyone's Obsessed With** — V26's 39.1% winner, untested on this video (the social-proof frame the merge abandoned)
3. **One Codex Session Now Runs All the Others** — concrete-mechanic frame, describes the director structure literally; complements (does not restate) a "Codex Runs Itself"-type thumbnail

**Hard rules:** discount the R1 leader 3–5pt when projecting (~33%); do NOT reuse "Nobody Knew They Needed" on non-Anthropic videos; don't run three variants of the same "The New Codex Update..." opener again — the frame must vary; if R2 titles stay flat, the thumbnail (not the wording) is the ceiling.

**Channel-level view performance (the verdict): the video flopped on VIEWS despite winning on watch-share.**
- **Ranked 10 of 10** in the channel's recent panel. **3,488 views** in a window where the channel typically gets **5,300–11,300** — YouTube's own card: **"34% fewer views,"** "subscribers choosing to watch this less often," and critically **"Fewer views from YouTube recommendations" (only 2,608 from Home/Up-next).**
- **CTR 9.3% (down arrow)** — below channel norm but NOT catastrophic.
- **Average view duration 4:13 (UP, green)** — retention is *good*. People who clicked stayed and watched well.
- Same window leaderboard: /dream 42.8k, /workflows 15.2k, Subagents 2.0 14.3k, Ultra Plan 10.0k, **/goal (first Codex vid) 7.3k**, monitor 6.7k. This video did **3.5k — about half of /goal**, the channel's previous Codex benchmark.

**Key takeaways:**

- **The title test worked; the topic is the ceiling.** "Going Viral Right Now" won at 40% — a **5th confirmation** of V26's observable-social-proof frame ("The [New] [Product] Feature Going Viral Right Now"), and it beat the concrete-benefit control (31%) and a magnitude frame (29%) by ~9–11pt. Combined with AVD 4:13 (up), the title+content did their jobs. **The flop was upstream of the title** — not enough people chose to click in the first place, and the algorithm stopped recommending it.

- **Primary cause: topic/audience fit.** This is an **Anthropic/Claude-centric channel** (every top recent video is "Anthropic Just Dropped…"). This is the **2nd Codex video**, and it did **half the views of the 1st** (/goal 7.3k → 3.5k). Codex content draws a smaller slice of this audience, and a *second* Codex video compounded the fatigue.

- **Secondary cause: abstract pattern, not a namable feature.** /goal, /workflows, /dream are concrete, recognizable tokens. This video sells a **workflow *pattern*** ("director / chief of staff / threads managing threads") with no shiny named feature. Pattern/thesis content historically pulls far fewer views than single-feature content on this channel. "The Director" is a Ray-coined metaphor — the viewer can't tell what they'll get, so they don't click, so YouTube doesn't recommend it.

- **The thumbnail improvement helped watch-share but couldn't fix the click.** The 1→2 hierarchy ("The Director") is cleaner than R1's three-tile mush, but even a clean thumbnail can't make an abstract-orchestration Codex topic broadly appealing to a Claude audience.

**Verdict:** Not a title or thumbnail failure — a **topic-ceiling failure** (2nd Codex video + abstract pattern on a Claude-first channel). Ray's call to **move it into the course** is correct: it's strong evergreen teaching content (good retention, real ideas), just not a broad-appeal YouTube topic. **Keep the title "The New Codex Feature Going Viral Right Now"** (40%, validated frame) if it stays public.

**Banked rules:** (1) Codex videos cap well below Claude videos on this channel — treat as a smaller-audience format; (2) a *second consecutive* Codex video compounds the penalty (half the views of the first); (3) abstract workflow/pattern topics underperform concrete named features regardless of title quality; (4) observable-social-proof ("Going Viral Right Now") now 5/5 — it's the default Codex frame; (5) when CTR is down but AVD is *up* and recommendations cratered, the problem is topic demand, not the title — don't keep re-testing titles.
