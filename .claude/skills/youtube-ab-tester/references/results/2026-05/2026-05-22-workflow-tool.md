**Key takeaways:**

- **20.8pt total spread, but it's really "two at 40, one at 19.6."** YouTube auto-declared the winner after only ~3h — a fast call, almost certainly triggered by #3 cratering so hard the algorithm gained confidence early. The genuine signal: #3's frame is broken; #1 and #2 are a **statistical tie (0.4pt)** — R1 did not separate them.

- **#3 "Anthropic Hasn't Announced This Claude Code Feature Yet" cratered at 19.6% — a new all-time channel floor.** Previous worst was V15 "Stop [X]" at 20.7%, V23 "cheat code" at 27.6%. Diagnosis: **negation/absence framing**. The title is defined by what *hasn't* happened ("hasn't announced") and carries no release verb. "Just Dropped" signals the thing is real and usable *now*; "Hasn't Announced" signals vaporware/rumor — viewers won't click into something that may not exist yet. It also names no feature and promises no concrete payoff.

- **Critical contrast with V21 "Anthropic's New Ultrareview Is Coming" (37.3%)** — that title is *also* a not-yet-released angle, but "Is Coming" is **forward and active** ("it's on the way"), not a negation ("hasn't announced"). The 37.3% → 19.6% gap is the entire cost of phrasing an early/leak angle as an absence instead of an arrival. **New anti-pattern banked: negation-verb titles ("Hasn't", "Won't", "Isn't") — early/leak angles must use a forward verb.**

- **#1 "the Feature Everyone Needed" at 40.4%** — V14's "Feature Nobody Knew They Needed" formula (46.8%, all-time #2) with the curiosity dial swapped: discovery ("nobody knew they needed") → universal relevance ("everyone needed"). 40.4% vs V14's 46.8% = the "Everyone Needed" variant gives back ~6pt. **Discovery-curiosity ("you didn't even know you needed this") beats plain universal-relevance ("everyone needed this").** Note #1 names no feature at all — title says WHY, thumbnail (`/workflows`) says WHAT. Textbook title-thumbnail complementarity.

- **#2 "Anthropic Just Dropped Claude Code Workflows (It's Awesome)" tied the winner at 40% — despite carrying two documented anti-patterns.** It has (a) a **parenthetical** (V1, V11 anti-pattern) and (b) "(It's Awesome)" is a **vague evaluative phrase**, same family as "game-changer"/"insane" that flopped on V23 R2 (31.3%). Hitting 40% *with* both handicaps strongly implies the clean version — **"Anthropic Just Dropped Claude Code Workflows"** — has real upside above 40%. The news+feature-name frame is currently being suppressed by its own parenthetical.

- **R1 verdict:** we learned the floor (negation = death) but did not separate the two live frames. R2 must break the #1/#2 tie *and* de-handicap #2.
