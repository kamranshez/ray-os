**Key takeaways:**
- **"Going Viral Right Now" leads at 36%** — adds an urgency/timing marker that the other two lack. The lever in R2 is **observable urgency**: "going viral" is something a viewer can imagine seeing on a feed; "obsessed with" and "can't stop using" are claims about other people's internal states.
- **R1 winner regressed 39.1% → 33.7% (-5.4pt)** — textbook early-round optimism. Now documented on V18 (39.6%→37.2%), V23 (39%→31.4%), V25 (36.9%→33.8%), and here. Steady state for "Obsessed With" frame is ~33-34%, not 39%. Always discount R1 leaders by 3-5pt when projecting.
- **"Nobody Can Stop Using" floors at 30.3%** — abstract addiction frame loses to concrete observable social proof. Same family failure as V25 R2's "Every Power User Will Use" (31.3%): predictions/inferences about behavior underperform claims about what's *visibly happening*.
- **5.7pt spread** — real signal still, no plateau yet. Frame variety still doing work.
- **New insight — within the "social proof" family, observable > inferable.** "Going viral" (you can see it) > "obsessed with" (you have to take Ray's word for it) > "nobody can stop using" (you have to infer addiction from absence of stopping). For R3, prefer frames where the proof is *visible* not *attributed*.
- **Untested in R1/R2:** authority-anchor as pull-quote ("OpenAI Calls This Most Consequential"), defection frame ("Why I Stopped Using Claude Code"), concrete-proof frame ("14 Hours Straight"). Worth one more round with diverse frames before locking.

**Risk to flag for ship decision:** "Going Viral Right Now" has a half-life — by week 3 the temporal marker dates the title. If R3 lands a comparable winner without a "right now" hook, prefer the evergreen one. If urgency family wins again, plan to swap the title around 2026-05-19.

**Status:** R2 leader provisional. R3 should genuinely test a different lever (authority pull-quote OR concrete-proof) — running three more social-proof variants will compress the spread the way V25 R3 did (1pt plateau).
