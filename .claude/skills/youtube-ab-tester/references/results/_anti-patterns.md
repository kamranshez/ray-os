## Anti-Patterns (Never Do This)

Documented failures to avoid repeating. Reference these when generating new title/thumbnail options.

### Title Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Evidence | Why It Fails |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Parentheticals in titles** | Video 1: parenthetical variants lost; Video 11: "(After 1,600 Hours)" contributed to launch failure | Feels like an afterthought, adds visual clutter |
| **Educational framing ("Explained", "Taught Me", "Looks Like")** | Videos 7, 8: "Explained" at ~30%; Video 11: all 3 "reflective" titles failed | Doesn't create urgency or curiosity |
| **Personal framing without authority** | Video 10: 25.4%; Video 11: launch failure | "I/My" alone doesn't give viewers a reason to click |
| **FOMO/guilt framing ("You Missed", "You're Missing Out")** | Video 2: underperformed at 28.8–30.3% | Feels accusatory, turns viewers off |
| **Listing multiple features in title** | Video 5: 26%; Video 3: "Weekly:" format at 29.7% | Too much info, no single hook |
| **Pipe format ("Here's What Changed")** | Video 9: 31.4% | Feels clickbaity, splitting attention |
| **All-similar A/B test variants** | Video 11: all 3 used same reflective framing → all failed | If all options share the same frame, you can't find a winner |
| **"Stop [doing X]" accusatory framing** | Video 15: 20.7%; Video 14: 22.8%; Video 11 R3: 29.5% | Consistently worst performer category (20-29%). Even worse when stacked with parenthetical |

### Thumbnail Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Evidence | Why It Fails |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Vague time references ("1 Year Later...", "A Lot Has Changed")** | Video 4: 21.5%; Video 11: launch failure | No value promise, no curiosity |
| **Year labels ("My 2026 Workflow")** | Video 4: 27.1% vs "How I Code Now" at 51.5% | Present tense "Now" always beats date stamps |
| **Feature framing over personal ("How [Feature]..." vs "How I...")** | Video 6: "How Planning Works Now" at 32.2% vs Video 4: "How I Code Now" at 51.5% | Personal framing creates stronger identification |
