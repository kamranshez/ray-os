## Anti-Patterns (Never Do This)

Documented failures to avoid repeating. Reference these when generating new title/thumbnail options.

Reconciliation history: 2026-06-12 blind re-derivation (V1–V31), contradictions approved and applied by Ray same day. 2026-07-07 clean-room audit — sanitized raw data (titles/ranks/shares only, no prose) was re-analyzed by a blind agent and diffed against this doc; amendments applied and V32–V35 folded in. ### Title Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Evidence | Why It Fails |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Literal mechanism description (title explains what the feature literally does)** | V31 R2: "Subagents Can Now Spawn Their Own Subagents" 22.4% (last); V22 R2: "Now Asks a Stronger Model When It Gets Confused" 23.8%; V14 R2: "Stop Interrupting Claude Code (Do This Instead)" 22.8%; V5: feature-list title 26% | Mechanism is the thumbnail's job. Viewers click stakes ("Biggest Upgrade Yet"), not explanations. Loses by 10–18 pts every time it meets a stakes frame. (Note 2026-07-07: the 19.6% workflow title previously cited here was miscategorized — it's absence-negation, next row) |
| **Absence-negation / passive-evidence framing ("Hasn't Announced", "The Signs Say")** | V27-workflows R1: "Anthropic Hasn't Announced This Claude Code Feature Yet" 19.6% — **worst title in the archive**; V33-fable-return R4: "The Signs Say Fable 5 Will Return" 27.4% (last, -10.4pp vs actor-active winner) | "The thing doesn't exist yet / might happen" gives no payoff and no actor. Both failures share hedged, agentless construction. Fix: name the actor with an active verb — in the same R4 round, both "Anthropic Will Bring..." arms beat the passive arm by 7–10pp |
| **Educational framing ("Explained", "Taught Me", "Looks Like")** | Videos 7, 8: "Explained" at ~30%; Video 11: all 3 "reflective" titles failed | Doesn't create urgency or curiosity |
| **Personal framing without a payoff** | Video 10: 25.4%; Video 11: launch failure; V27-workflows R2: "my new favourite thing" 23.5% (last); V19 R3: "I Found..." 30.6% (last); V34-code-with-claude R1: "I Asked Anthropic Engineers How They REALLY Use Claude Code" 27.4% (last, -10.7pp vs same frame WITH payoff clause) | "I/My" alone doesn't give viewers a reason to click. Exception: personal-method videos where Ray is the subject AND the title carries a stakes/payoff clause (V4 won; V32-anki "Changed My Life (Seriously...)" won 2/2; V34 "Here's Their REAL Workflow" won). The payoff clause is the lever, not the first person |
| **Third-party credential on a personal-method video** | V32-anki R1: "How Top 1% Learners Use Claude Code to Study Faster" 27.6% (last, -10.8pp) | Credential framing ("Top 0.01%", "Anthropic engineers") wins on technique/thesis videos (V11, V24, V30) — but on a "how I do X" video, "how *they* do it" loses to "how *I* do it". Keep the speaker as the subject |
| **FOMO/guilt framing ("You Missed", "You're Missing Out")** | Video 2: underperformed at 28.8–30.3% | Feels accusatory, turns viewers off |
| **Listing multiple features in title** | Video 5: 26%; Video 3: "Weekly:" format at 29.7% | Too much info, no single hook |
| **Pipe format ("Here's What Changed")** | Video 9: 31.4%; V26: "\| The Verdict" 26.1% (last) | Feels clickbaity, splitting attention |
| **All-similar A/B test variants** | Video 11: all 3 used same reflective framing → all failed; V16 R3 and V17 R1: three same-family paraphrases → 33/33/33 noise rounds; V33-fable-return R1: three "coming back / prepare" variants → 2.1pp spread, R2 went frame-diverse and blew open to 10pp | If all options share the same frame, you can't find a winner — the round measures nothing |
| **"Stop [doing X]" accusatory framing** | Video 15: 20.7%; Video 14: 22.8%; Video 11 R3: 29.5%; Video 11 R2: 32.2% — 0 wins, 4 losses | Consistently worst performer category (20-29%). Even worse when stacked with parenthetical |
| **Secrecy words and bare leak tags ("Secret", "Secretly", "Hidden", "(Leak!)")** | V18 R1: "a Secret Feature" 28.8% (last); V18 R2: "Secretly Added" 26.9% (last); V19 R3: "Hidden in Source Code" 30.6% (last); V33-fable-return R2: "(Leak!)" 28.8% (last, on a video literally about a leak) | Placed last 4/4 times. A leak *event* can carry a video (V19); a secrecy/leak *label* reads as low-substance. NOT the same as "Nobody Knew They Needed" — that's a usefulness-surprise claim and it wins |
| **Transplanting a winning title onto a topic-mismatched video** | V28 R1: "Anthropic Just Dropped the Biggest Subagent Upgrade Yet" (V25's winner, verbatim) placed last at 30.3% on a workflows video — then won V31 (a subagent video) at 41% two weeks later | The phrase wasn't fatigued, it was misapplied. Verbatim reuse only on topic-matched videos |
| **"From You" / "Than You" you-vs-them suffixes** | V27 R2: "Creator Prompts Differently From You" last at 29.4% despite Boris authority anchor | Comparative framing against the viewer reads accusatory — soft Stop-X adjacency |
| **Intensifiers stacked on a winning negation ("Even...Anymore", "Already", "Completely")** | V27 R3: "Isn't Prompting" 35.3% → "Isn't Even Prompting Anymore" 27.7% (-7.6pp, same root title) | The negation IS the lever; intensifiers read as trying too hard |

### Thumbnail Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Evidence | Why It Fails |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Vague time references ("1 Year Later...", "A Lot Has Changed")** | Video 4: 21.5%; Video 11: launch failure | No value promise, no curiosity |
| **Year labels ("My 2026 Workflow")** | Video 4: 27.1% vs "How I Code Now" at 51.5% | Present tense "Now" always beats date stamps |
| **Feature framing over personal ("How [Feature]..." vs "How I...")** | Video 6: "How Planning Works Now" at 32.2% vs Video 4: "How I Code Now" at 51.5% | Personal framing creates stronger identification |
| **Plain folder icon when an annotated composition is available (post-V20)** | V22: folder 30.5% vs command-list 41.1%; V25: folder 33.6% vs browser composition 35.1%; V20: /ultraplan folder 35.3% lost to faceless "Plan Mode 2.0." 38.5%; V35-fable-sunset R2: "Soon..." folder 30.2% (last) | Folder icons won in March (V18, V19) but have lost every head-to-head against richer annotated compositions since April 2026 |
| **Text-remix or reskin challengers against an established incumbent thumbnail** | V30-loops R4: incumbent 38.8% vs two fresh challengers 30.9%/30.3%; V35-fable-sunset R2: incumbent two-icon 37.3% vs orange recolor of the SAME layout 32.5% | Incumbents accumulate impression history; a recolor/reskin is not a new concept, it reads as a worse-contrast version of the familiar image. Only a different composition CLASS has dethroned one (V22 thumb R2 before/after diagram) |
| **Recycling a recent video's winning thumbnail on a companion video** | V35-fable-sunset reused V33's two-icon "DON'T WASTE IT" thumb: won within-test (37.3%) yet the video threw "fewer subscribers choosing to watch" at ~4h and ranked 9/10 on views | Familiarity wins the within-test click among variants but reads as "already watched this" to subscribers — the incumbent look is the ceiling, not the winner. A burned layout needs a genuinely different concept, not a variant |

### Ceiling Anti-Patterns (topic and format — two DISTINCT effects, split 2026-07-07)

| Pattern | Evidence | Why It Fails |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Thesis/paradigm-format videos (no single named feature)** | V27 WTF Are Loops 6.3k vs 8.4k–18k typical (CTR-flag at 90 min); March 2026 data: thesis avg ~17K vs ~45K single-feature. Cleanest proof: V15 locked a 49.4% title winner — highest share in archive — and STILL underperformed views at every checkpoint. Also V11 (tips compilation): 55% fewer views at launch, still 43% fewer after 5 title rounds | "Fewer subscribers choosing to watch" fires early → recommendations collapse. Package optimization cannot fix it (V27: 4 A/B rounds, settled package, still capped). Topic class sets the ceiling; titles only redistribute share within it. Smuggle the thesis inside a single-feature video instead — the feature is the hook, the paradigm is the payload |
| **Non-Anthropic topic ceiling (Codex on a Claude channel) — compounds back-to-back** | V-codex-goal (2026-05-05): a normal /goal FEATURE video, not thesis — still capped ~7.3k in a window where /dream hit 42.8k; V29 codex-director (2026-06-01, second consecutive Codex video): ~3.5k, roughly half again, "fewer subscribers" flag at 90 min | This is a TOPIC penalty, independent of format — a feature-format Codex video still capped. Consecutive off-topic videos compound the hit. Spacing rule: don't schedule Codex/non-Anthropic videos back-to-back, and don't expect packaging to fix the cap (V29 was previously mis-filed under thesis-format; reclassified 2026-07-07) |

### Downgraded (was never-do, now judgment call)

| Pattern | Evidence | Current read |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Parentheticals in titles** | Against: V1 parenthetical variants lost; V11 "(After 1,600 Hours)" part of a failed launch; V14 "(Do This Instead)" 22.8% stacked on Stop-X; V33-fable-return R2 "(Leak!)" 28.8% last; V33 R3 (provisional): "(Get Ready Now)" DRAGGED a strong headline (-4pp when dropped). For: V27-workflows R1 "(It's Awesome)" 40.0%; V32-anki "(Seriously...)" won 2/2 (38.4% → 45.4%, auto-declared) | Judge the payload. A self-aware credibility hedge ("(Seriously...)") wins; a low-substance tag ("(Leak!)") loses; a directive urgency tag ("(Get Ready Now)") is a crutch for a weak field that turns into dead weight once the headline is strong. The parenthetical isn't the killer — weak content inside it is |
| **Naming-the-paradigm titles on thesis videos ("X Are the New Y")** | V30-loops R4: "Loops Are the New Prompts" last at 30.3% vs 38.8% — but R4 was a mixed-variable round: this title ran on a brand-new thumbnail while the winner kept the incumbent, and incumbents beat mid-flight challengers regardless of title | Hypothesis never cleanly tested, not falsified-confirmed. If it matters, re-test with the thumbnail held constant |
