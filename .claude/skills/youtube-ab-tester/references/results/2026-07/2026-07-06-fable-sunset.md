## Key takeaways

- **Subscriber-skip ≠ within-test share.** R2 "picked the least-bad" of three, but the winner is the *recycled* look — and the video still capped at rank 9/10 with the "fewer subscribers choosing to watch" flag. Within-test share optimization can't fix an external topic/familiarity ceiling (banked V29/V30 lesson).
- **The reused v33 thumbnail is the ceiling, not the winner.** It wins *within* the test because it's high-contrast and familiar; that same familiarity is why subscribers skip (looks like the v33 "Coming Back" video they already watched 10 days prior).
- **Reskins don't reset a burned thumbnail.** Neither a folder nor an orange recolor broke the pattern. A genuinely different *concept* was never tested — countdown/expiry (hourglass, "3 DAYS LEFT") and the "where it matters" heatmap (which actually pairs with the winning title) remain untested.
- **Deadline titles work; specificity tail wins.** "Ends This Week + Here's Where It Actually Matters" > soft "Last Cheap Days (Spend Them Right)".
