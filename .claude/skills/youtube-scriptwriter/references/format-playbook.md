# Format playbook

What performs on the channel and why, from VidTempla analytics + retention curves + transcripts of 37 videos (Nov 2025 to Jul 2026). Full analysis: `artefacts/youtube-format-analysis-2026-07.md`. Raw numbers (full ranked table, retention curves, per-video format ledger): `references/performance-data.md` in this skill. Use this file to pick and justify angles in Round 1 of the interview and to engineer retention into the draft; dip into the raw data when a specific comparison is needed.

## The four format tiers

### Tier 1: urgency/event-framed strategy (the current best format)

Strategy or workflow substance wearing a time-boxed wrapper: a deadline, a launch, a window closing.

- "Fable 5 Is Back Tomorrow! (Don't Waste It)": #1 views/day (1,249), best intro survival on the channel (70% alive at the 5% mark, no cliff). Script: `uploaded/fable-5-where-to-point-it.md`.
- "You Only Have 1 Day Left With Fable 5": #4 views/day, 11.9% finish rate. Script: `uploaded/fable-exit-interview.md`.

Why it wins: urgency earns the click like a news title, but the strategy body holds viewers the way news bodies don't. Both the click AND the intro survive.

### Tier 2: news volume plays ("Anthropic Just Dropped")

Still the raw-reach engine (best: 100k views, +1,751 subs) but structurally leaky and fatiguing:

- 65-70% of clickers gone by the 10% mark, every time. Broad curiosity traffic bounces regardless of script quality.
- Reuse fatigue is measurable: the June rerun of "Biggest Subagent Upgrade Yet" did 225 views/day vs the April original's 305 at nearly triple the age; 15th+ uses of the title formula crowd the bottom half of the rankings.
- Survivors rewatch heavily (relative performance climbs to 0.46-0.71 by the end): the bodies function as reference material for practitioners.

Use when the news is genuinely big; differentiate the body or it is a commodity video.

### Tier 3: insider/workflow/identity (best watch quality and conversion, under-distributed)

- "I Spent a Day With Anthropic Engineers": channel-best retention shape (313s avg duration, 13.2% finish, relative performance rarely below 0.48). Script: `uploaded/day-with-anthropic-engineers.md`.
- "Loop Engineering": best early hold on the channel (relative performance peaks 0.76 at the 15% mark). Script: `uploaded/wtf-are-loops.md`.
- "My Claude Code Workflow for 2026": 3.3% sub conversion vs 1.7% for the 100k news hit.

For these, packaging is the bottleneck, not the script. Pair with a Tier 1 wrapper when possible.

### Tier 4: pure explainers with no hook (avoid)

"Fully Explained", "Highest Point of Leverage", "Task System Explained", feature roundups: bottom third. They lose the door (20% alive at 10%) and the middle (relative performance 0.09 at the 62% mark). If the material is an explainer, find it a stakes wrapper or an insider discovery frame before scripting.

## The skeleton every winner shares

1. Cold open: time-stamp or stakes claim, subject named within two sentences, explicit promise or roadmap. Zero branding.
2. First-principles recap with a drawn mental model.
3. Live demo on Ray's own real products (Hyperwhisper, RayOS, the masterclass itself), exact prompt spoken verbatim. Signature move: start the run, teach the internals while it runs, return for the payoff.
4. Insider evidence as differentiator: binary digs, proxy-extracted prompts, file-system spelunking, N=10 experiments, named-engineer quotes. Always converted into a transferable principle ("even if you can't access the feature, apply the pattern") and often a downloadable artifact.
5. Use-case fan-out with explicit decision heuristics and objection handling ("now you may be thinking...").
6. A sustained analogy spine (REM sleep, security camera, chief of staff, Minecraft creative mode), not one-off decoration.
7. Personal verdict with honest limitations.
8. Prediction/future tease routed to the newsletter; comment/email ask as the closer.

## Retention rules (from the channel's own curves)

- The first 35 seconds carry the video; broad clickers decide by the 10% mark. Proof or the finished thing on screen immediately.
- The 30-60% zone is the danger zone. Undifferentiated use-case fan-outs collapse there (one video fell from 0.63 to 0.28 relative performance across 30-48%). Plant a delayed payoff (experiment result, demo return, verdict) in that zone.
- Rewatch spikes align with dense on-screen artifacts: exact prompts, configs, diagrams. Give viewers things worth pausing on, especially in the back half.
- Strong finishes (11-13% of viewers reaching the end) correlate with a verdict + future tease + personal note, not a hard outro.

## CTA economy

- Mid-video at a concept boundary, woven into the content. Best case: the demo literally implements or references the offer (a workflow that adds the sale banner, an async agent researching "why take my masterclass").
- Proof stock phrases in rotation: "most comprehensive class online", "under 0.2% refunds", "months ahead of the curve", "12 hours every single day", plus urgency (sale, lifetime removal) and pre-handled objections.
- Trust-sell register (used on the Anki and /goal videos): no hard pitch at all, "I only upload when there's something worth saying", newsletter only. Anki still did 435 views/day. Use for passion/workflow topics where a pitch would poison the sincerity.

## Length

1,500-3,800 words, 8-20 min. Strategy/insider at the long end, single-feature news at the short end.
