## Retention curves (top 6 and bottom of the range)

- **r4_KLZvHoaA "Fable 5 Is Back Tomorrow!" (#1)** — Best intro survival of the set: watchRatio 0.70 at 5%, 0.57 at 10%. No cliff, smooth decay. Rewatch bumps at 44%, 52%, 66%, 72%, 75%, 84%, 91%. Ends at 9.1%.
- **OnQ4BGN8B-s "Feature Nobody Knew They Needed" (#2, 100k)** — Steep intro cliff: 0.57 at 5% but 0.35 by 10%. Relative performance starts 0.16-0.19 (broad clickbait traffic bounces) then rises to 0.60 at 43%. Ends 8.5% with relPerf 0.46: survivors are reference-viewers.
- **c0gVowvMR-g "Dynamic Workflows news" (#3)** — Brutal 1-3% cliff: 0.89 to 0.37; 0.33 at 5%. Constant skip-back/rewatch spikes; relPerf climbs to 0.71 by the end. Ends 10.4%. Two videos in one: casual clickers bounce, practitioners study it.
- **B1sYKOWVHR0 "1 Day Left With Fable 5" (#4)** — 0.567 at 5%, 0.528 at 10%. Dozens of small rewatch bumps throughout. Ends 11.9%, exceptional finish.
- **2-0lxK2wgJ8 "Loop Engineering" (#5)** — 0.505 at 5%, plateau after. RelPerf peaks 0.76 at 15-16%, the best early hold on the channel. Mid-sag around 50-52% (0.39) with recovery at 55-56% and 88-93%. Ends 11.4%.
- **hMgB1bjkI7o "Day With Anthropic Engineers" (#6)** — 0.526 at 5%. RelPerf peaks 0.70 at 13%, rarely below 0.48 anywhere. Ends 13.2%, best end retention measured. Highest AVD in top group (313s).
- **kJ8FTPykawU "Dynamic Workflows (Fully Explained)" (#29)** — Worst intro: 0.25 at 5%, 0.20 at 10%. Mid-video craters to relPerf 0.087 at 62%. Ends 3.1%. The pure explainer without an urgency hook died at the door and in the middle.
- **i4fMF1pug3w "Biggest Subagent Upgrade Yet (Jun rerun)" (#15)** — Decent intro (0.52 at 5%) but relPerf collapses 0.63 to 0.28 across 30-48% (undifferentiated use-case fan-out), recovering to 0.72 at 74-79%. Title-formula reuse fatigue: the April original did 305/day at 81 days old.
