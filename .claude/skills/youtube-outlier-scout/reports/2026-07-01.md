### Format Gap -- Outliers vs Our Channel

The "what are they doing that we're not" section. This week the topic (Sonnet 5) is one
everyone shares -- so the breakouts are separated by FORMAT, which is exactly Ray's gap.

**Biggest outliers this period (by channel-median multiplier + notable raw):**
1. **"Google Just Dropped a Masterclass on Agentic Engineering (It's SO Good)"** -- @ColeMedin (213k subs, 90.9k views = 0.43x v/s, 3.6x median) -- format: **review/curation of an external resource.** Why it worked: borrows Google's authority, positions Cole as the curator who distills it. We publish "Anthropic Just Dropped X" (first-party news) but never "here's a great thing someone ELSE made, distilled." 0% of our mix.
2. **"I asked Claude Code to make me as much money as possible"** -- @nateherk (831k subs, 111.5k views = 0.13x v/s, 1.1x median) -- format: **challenge / outcome-transformation.** Why it worked: open-ended ambition + a number payoff the viewer wants to see resolved. We make this 0% of the time.
3. **Sonnet-5 test/verdict wave** -- @intheworldofai, @Chase-H-AI (both ~0.5-1.0x their median, but a real-time surge on a fresh launch) -- format: **review/verdict (test).** Why it worked: "is the new model actually good?" is the highest-intent question the day a model drops. We cover launches as NEWS, not as a tested verdict.

**Our current format mix (last ~20 videos):** news ~70% / tutorial ~15% / thesis-concept ~10% / personal ~5% / **test/battle 0% / review-verdict 0% / build-demo ~0% / challenge 0%.**

Ray's own non-news history says the gap is real money: his outliers are non-news formats
(the 331k Anki how-to, the 54k Replit review). He wins when he leaves the news lane and
still almost never does it.

**Formats winning for others that we under-use:**
- **Review / verdict (test)** -- 3+ channels this week; we publish it ~0%. Travels because
  "is it actually good?" is evergreen + search-friendly, and it works on a launch day
  without being pure news.
- **Challenge / outcome-transformation** -- nateherk's 111k; we publish it 0%. Open-ended
  ambition + a payoff number pulls curiosity beyond the sub base.
- **Curation of an external resource** -- ColeMedin's 3.6x; we publish it ~0%. Borrows
  someone else's authority, low production cost, high perceived value.
