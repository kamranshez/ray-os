### Title Patterns This Week

**Cross-channel patterns (strongest signals):**

- **"Harness" is the word of the week** -- seen across 4+ channels. @aiDotEngineer's
  top outlier is literally "Harnesses in AI: A Deep Dive" (6.9x). @ColeMedin posted
  "Anthropic Just Dropped a Masterclass on Building Agent Harnesses". @aarondfrancis
  posted "The coding harness for your coding harness". Claude's own channel posted
  "Building the best agentic analytics harness". Anthropic seeded the term and the
  niche is amplifying it.
- **"Agentic OS" framing** -- @Itssssss_Jack used "(Agentic OS)" as a parenthetical
  on two uploads, Jack Roberts copied it verbatim ("Claude Code just got 10X Better
  (Agentic OS)"), and @BenAI92 ran "Build an AI Operating System Like The 1%". The
  "operating system" metaphor for agent stacks is spreading.
- **Codex feature wave** -- OpenAI shipped a batch (goals, Appshots, plugins) and
  creators are reacting fast: @rileybrownai "Codex Mobile App", @nateherk "Use Your
  Claude Code Projects in Codex", "Codex: Build Your Full AI Marketing Team". Codex
  is having a loud product week.

**Title formulas working right now:**

1. **"[Topic]: A Deep Dive"** -- plain, authoritative, no hype. The single biggest
   watchlist outlier (6.9x) used exactly this. Counter-signal to clickbait fatigue.
2. **Contrarian question + soft-walk-back** -- Cal Newport's "Has AI Conquered
   Coding? (It's Not So Simple...)". Question hook plus a parenthetical that promises
   nuance.
3. **Money/outcome promise + urgency** -- "Will Make Millionaires (do this now)",
   "How to Sell Claude Code to Local Businesses (START HERE)". Business-of-AI angle
   with a directive parenthetical.
4. **"Build [X] That [does hard thing] Without [failure mode]"** -- "Build Agents
   That Run for Hours (Without Losing the Plot)" (5.0x). Names the aspiration and the
   fear in one line.
5. **News-reaction framing** -- "What Karpathy Joining Anthropic Actually Means For
   Claude" (@nateherk, 104k). Tie a personal channel angle to a real industry event.
