Fable 5 is coming back. People have spotted changes in the Claude Code binary before any announcement.

And when it lands, most people will point it at their whole codebase and say "fix the bugs," hoping for magic.

That's how you burn a weekly limit in a few hours.

The model roams free, runs out of context, fixes a few leaves, and you're left buying usage credits to keep going.

The engineers who get the most out of it do the opposite. They spend a cheap model first.

Here's the technique that even Anthropic engineers use:
→ Sweep every file in the codebase with a cheap model (Sonnet, Opus)
→ Score each section 1 to 5 for the task at hand
→ Throw out the 1s and 2s
→ Point the expensive model only at the top of the list

The bottleneck was never the model's intelligence.

It was you aiming it at everything instead of at the one place that mattered.

I talk more about this in the video linked in the comments 👇
