People are buying and selling human faces just to get access to Claude.

Let that tell you how badly the world wants the best models.

China is officially cut off from Claude. So an entire underground economy grew up to reach it anyway.

At first it was simple. Developers just routed their requests through Singapore to look like they were somewhere else.

Then in April 2026, Anthropic raised the wall. For some users, a government ID and a live selfie became required.

But that didn't slow developers in China down.

First they used deepfakes, fake faces good enough to pass the selfie check.

And when that wasn't enough, recruiters started flying into lower-income countries across Africa and Latin America. They find real people and pay them under $30 to scan their own face and ID on camera, so developers thousands of miles away can use Claude models.

When a tool is good enough, people will move heaven, earth, and even their identity to reach it.
