Mythos hasn't released publicly yet and Anthropic is already telling you how it's going to fit into your workflow.

Not as your main model. As your advisor.

The /advisor command that just shipped lets you configure a reviewer model separate from the executor. When the main model gets stuck, the advisor gets everything (the full session, every tool call, every result) and sends back feedback.

There's a specific feeling when Claude Code goes in circles on something hard. It's not frustration. It's the slow realization the model has been confidently wrong for 15 minutes and you weren't sure when it crossed the line.

That's the exact problem this is designed to solve.

But what I find interesting is when Anthropic says to call the advisor: before substantive work, before committing to an approach, and when declaring the task done.

That's not a recovery tool. That's a quality gate. And eventually, Mythos will be sitting behind it.
