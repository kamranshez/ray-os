Your Claude Code skills have an expiration date.

Anthropic just quietly admitted this. They built a benchmarking tool into the Skill Creator that lets you A/B test any skill against the base model.

Why? Because every time a new model drops, some of your skills become redundant. The capability you painstakingly encoded is now baked into the weights.

Opus 4.6 may not be able to do great SEO audits (without a skill). But Opus 5 might. When it does, your SEO audit skill isn't helping, it's holding the model back.

The move: after every model upgrade, re-run your critical skills through the A/B test. Keep what helps. Delete what doesn't.

The best skill developers will be the ones who know when to kill their own work.
