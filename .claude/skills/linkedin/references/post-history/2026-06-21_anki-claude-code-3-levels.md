Hardly anyone is using Claude Code with Anki yet. Here are the 3 levels of it.

𝗟𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹 𝟭: Make the cards

Point Claude Code at a YouTube lecture, a textbook, or a video you watched. It pulls the transcript, grabs the relevant diagrams, generates new ones, and builds the deck. Ordered beginner to advanced.

𝗟𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹 𝟮: Close the loop

This is where it gets powerful. Claude reads your actual review history through Anki Connect, the cards you mark good and the ones you mark again, and decides what to fix, reprioritize, or delete. Your real data drives the next move.

𝗟𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹 𝟯: Check you're applying it

This is the level almost no one reaches. Hook Claude up to whatever you actually do. Meeting transcripts, the emails you send, even the chess games you play. It cross-checks your real behavior against your deck and surfaces the cards you can recall but never apply, so you stop confusing knowing with doing.

The decision rule:
→ Want a deck fast → level 1
→ Want it to stay sharp → level 2
→ Want it to show up in real life → level 3

Most people haven't found level 1 yet. Level 3 is where it gets unfair.
