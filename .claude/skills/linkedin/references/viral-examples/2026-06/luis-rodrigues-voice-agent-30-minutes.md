Claude just built a voice agent for my site.

I wanted to see how fast I could get one working.

Not a demo.
Not a prototype.
A real agent who knows my product.

Turns out: 30 minutes.

I pasted my URL into PolyAI's agent builder.
It scraped my entire site.

Came back with 27 topics and 22 functions I never configured.

Voice personality.
Safety filters.
All auto-detected.

Then I pulled the whole thing into Claude Code.
Added GDPR compliance.
Tweaked the responses.

Pushed it live from Claude.

Here's the setup if you want to try it:

1. Go to poly.ai → Click "Build an agent."
2. Paste your website. It reads your FAQs, policies, and product info.
3. Test it in the browser. Talk to your agent before anything goes live.
4. Install the PolyAI ADK and pull the agent into your IDE.
5. Open Claude Code. Edit, fix, push, straight to production.

The part that surprised me most:

I didn't configure a single topic manually.
The agent knew more about my own product than my FAQ page does.
