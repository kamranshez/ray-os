The evidence base behind the formula in `post-formats.md`. Two sources: Matt
Pocock's (@mattpocockuk) video posts, ranked by likes, and the guinnesschen
Codex director-pattern announcement that kicked this off. Ranked by engagement,
not follower count, so this is *what landed*. Read this file when grading a
post on check-back, or when adding a new high-performer to the receipts.

## Grading a post on check-back
Grade against Ray's own recent distribution, never against Matt Pocock's absolute
counts (different account size). Looking good = top quartile of his recent posts
or an unusually high bookmark rate. Around his median = the format worked, move
on. Below median = name why (payoff given away, weak hook, sale stacked, no
close at all) and fold the lesson back into these reference files if it is a
real pattern, not a one-off.

## Growing the benchmark
When a new high-performing video post worth learning from appears (Ray's own or
someone else's), add it to the receipts here, and if it reveals a new move, add
that to the formats in `post-formats.md`. Ray's own logged posts in
`socials/x/promo-posts-log.md` are the evidence for which formats earn the watch
for his account specifically; settle open questions (like reaction close vs
colon handoff) from that log, not from intuition.
